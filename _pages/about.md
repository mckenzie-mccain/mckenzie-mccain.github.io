Howdy! My name is McKenzie McCain, and I am a student at Texas A&M University studying Robotics and Bionics. My interests revolve around developing robotic systems, including medical applications for robots and biomimetic robots to enhance human functionality as well as improving the quality of lives. 

I am a hatchling director for one of the largest project-based organizations at Texas A&M, [TURTLE Robotics](https://www.turtlerobotics.org/development-programs), where I manage 200+ college students from various organizations and also led the design and production of universal controllers for Hatchling competition robots (H.U.R.C). I also work on the research and development of an electromyographic-controlled prosthetic hand for a local boy through TURTLE [(OLSN)](https://www.turtlerobotics.org/projects/olsn) and a human hand gripper for [CtrlRobot](https://zh.engr.tamu.edu/).

## Research Interests
* **Biomimetic Robotics:** Designing bio-inspired robotic systems that replicate human and animal biomechanics to improve dexterity, adaptability, and efficiency in real-world tasks.
* **Human-Machine Interface:** Developing intuitive control interfaces that translate user intent into reliable robot behavior through sensing, signal processing, and responsive feedback.
* **Exoskeleton Control:** Creating control strategies for exoskeletons that provide adaptative assistance based on user motion and intent to improve strength, mobility and stability.
