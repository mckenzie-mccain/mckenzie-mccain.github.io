I am a hatchling director for one of the largest project-based organizations at Texas A&M, [TURTLE Robotics](https://www.turtlerobotics.org/development-programs), where I manage 200+ college students from various organizations and also led the design and production of universal controllers for Hatchling competition robots (H.U.R.C). I also work on the research and development of an electromyographic-controlled prosthetic hand for a local boy through TURTLE [(OLSN)](https://www.turtlerobotics.org/projects/olsn) and a human hand gripper for [CtrlRobot](https://zh.engr.tamu.edu/).
